# PLAN.md

> From PRODUCT.md section 7 and `product-thinking/05-v1.md`. Riskiest part first, in its simplest form. The plan will change as founders use it. Dates are a guess **[guess, review]**.

Landing page first, one hour: the first-screen words from DESIGN.md section 5 (headline, line under it, button) and the four fields. Stop at the hour.

1. I can enter one real company and read 3 emails to real investors that I would send myself (the investors hardcoded). **now**
2. I can see 20 investors ranked from a seeded list of 100, a reason for each, and a retry message if it takes over 15 seconds.
3. I can edit an email and open it prefilled in my Gmail, or copy it if the link fails.
4. I can enter a sector with no match and get a clear message, not a junk list.
5. I can mark an investor "sent" and see it in the tracker.
6. I can see follow-ups drafted for investors I emailed five days ago.
7. I can mark a reply and that investor's follow-ups stop.
8. A stranger can finish the whole flow without me explaining it, and the step log shows each step reached.

Last: I can close it, reopen it, and my data is still there.

Compressed schedule **[guess, review]**: Tue 6 Oct milestones 1 to 3; Wed 7 Oct milestones 4 to 6 and put it in front of 3 founders; Thu 8 Oct milestones 7, 8 and the last one, fix what founders broke; Fri 9 Oct no new scope.

# Landing page spine (draft for Anurag, 13 Oct 2026)

Rule for every block: one idea, as few words as possible, one action. A first-time founder should know in 5 seconds what this is, who it's for and what to tap.

Research basis (2026 SaaS landing guides): outcome headline, one main CTA, proof near the top, problem → how it works → proof → offer → FAQ → final CTA. Sources: saashero.net (effective SaaS landing page structure), designrevision.com (B2B SaaS landing patterns), peerpush.com (indie landing guide).

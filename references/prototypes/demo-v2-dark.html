FAQ

Questions, answered

Where does the investor data come from?

Funds' and angels' own websites, public deal news, and investors who claim their profiles. Every record shows its sources and the date it was last checked.

Do you send emails for me?

No. You send from your Gmail. If you connect Gmail, we only read threads with investors on your list, to update your pipeline.

What does "verified" mean?

Three levels: researched (sourced from public pages), checked (reviewed by our team in the last 90 days), and claimed (confirmed by the investor).

Is my raise visible to the public?

No. Your profile and round are shared only with investors you email or verified investors whose thesis fits, and you can turn that off.

What does it cost?

Free during beta for founders. Always free for investors.

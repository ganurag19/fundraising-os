FAQ

Questions, answered

I've never raised before. Is this for me?

Yes. It's built for first rounds: pre-seed and seed, ₹25L to ₹10 Cr, in India. It tells you what to do next, one step at a time.

Where does the investor information come from?

Investors' own websites, public news about their deals, and investors who confirm their profiles. Every fact shows where it came from and when we last checked.

Do you send emails for me?

No. You send from your own Gmail. If you connect Gmail, we only read emails with investors on your list, to keep your pipeline up to date.

How are investors rated?

Only founders who emailed or met an investor through us can rate them, on replies, feedback, respect and whether they'd pitch again. Investors can reply to reviews.

Is this a fundraising platform?

No. We're a research and outreach assistant. We don't list your company publicly, take part in your deal, or handle money.

What does it cost?

Free during beta for founders. Always free for investors.
